						--Commando--

Armed with a Laser-Designated Rocket Launcher. Unlike in TFC, there is no rocket-jumping. Also armed with a Plasma Rifle and Anti-Personnel Grenades (plasma nades.) Also armed with a Combat Shield which can be used at any time by a right click.

--MECHANICS--
HP:200
SPEED:200

Bullrush - Allows the Commando to bull-rush enemies with his Power Shield. Automatically moves you forward and only allows you to sidestep when activated. If the player collides with a wall, his HP is reduced by 25, the player is slowed down.If the player collides with another player, he is dealt fatal damage of 200 HP. If this player has more than 200 HP, he is knocked back. If the player blocks an attack with a shield, they deal 100 damage + knockback. Also has a reload timer next to the ammo icons.

AutoBoot - Automatic boot kick melee. Kicks enemies down onto the ground, forces them to get up. Also deals 25 damage. After 1 hit, it cooldowns for 10 seconds. Does not work on oversized Escort and Support targets.

Adrenaline Rush - Activates an Adrenalin Rush which can be fueled by killing enemies. Boosts player speed and damage resistance. 2 enemies killed - 1 second of use. Maximum cast time - 20 seconds. Also, when used, redness appears on the edges of the screen.

Battle Cry - Commando emits a battle cry that gives himself and any teammates who hear it an Adrenalin Rush. It lasts for 20 seconds, after which it goes into a cooldown.

--Speical Techtree unlocks--
Rallyflag - Gives nearby teammates an adrenalin rush if in range.
Target Info - View enemy health and name when hovering over them with your crosshair.

Commando's grenades that he takes from his belt with the desired animation.In total, there should be as many grenades in the inventory as on the pois. Grenades should dynamically disappear from the poise when dropped.When thrown, they leave green smoke behind them. In flight, they can be deflected with a shield.

Combat shield:
Different for different teams. For humans, it is a small device that opens into a large shield when pressed. It can withstand 100 HP damage. When the 100 HP limit is exceeded, it turns off and goes to cooldown for 30 seconds. If it is close to being turned off, its lightning is colored red. For aliens, it is a hologram, which also glows red when the amount of HP is small. For both shields, parrying as a quick melee is available.
